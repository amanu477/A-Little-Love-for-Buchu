Build a beautiful, highly polished, interactive romantic website dedicated to my girlfriend.

## IMPORTANT CONTEXT

Her name is **Abugu**, and I call her **Buchu**.

This website is a surprise for her after we had a small uncomfortable conversation. We did NOT have a serious fight, breakup, cheating, or major mistake. We simply disagreed, I got a little angry, and things became slightly uncomfortable.

Therefore, DO NOT make this feel like a desperate apology website.

The emotional message should be:

> "We had a little uncomfortable moment, but you're still my favorite person, I still love you, and I wanted to make you smile."

The website should make her:

1. Smile

5. Feel that the website was genuinely made specifically for her

# DESIGN DIRECTION

Create a premium, elegant, modern romantic experience.

Style:

* Soft
* Cute
* Playful
* Romantic
* Slightly funny
* Elegant
* Emotional without being overly dramatic

Avoid:

* Excessive hearts everywhere
* Cheap-looking Valentine's templates
* Overly dramatic apology language
* Huge amounts of text
* Childish cartoon design
* Generic stock imagery
* Anything that looks like a template

Use a sophisticated color palette such as:

* warm cream / ivory
* soft blush pink
* muted rose
* deep burgundy
* dark charcoal

Use beautiful modern typography with an elegant serif font for major headings and a clean sans-serif font for body text.

The website must look excellent on BOTH mobile and desktop, with mobile being the priority because she will probably open it on her phone.

# EXPERIENCE

Make the website feel like a small interactive story rather than a normal website.

The flow should be:

1. Intro
2. Meet Buchu
3. The "evidence" section
4. Photo memories
5. Things I love about her
6. Our little disagreement — handled playfully
7. Interactive forgiveness/relationship game
8. Emotional final message
9. Surprise ending

Use smooth scrolling, subtle animations, parallax where appropriate, floating elements, elegant transitions, hover effects, and tasteful micro-interactions.

Do NOT over-animate everything.

Animations should feel premium.
